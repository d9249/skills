# Cyber-capable model release audits

Use with primary launch pages, full system cards/PDFs, current access/retention documentation and vendor disclosure evidence. Review public evidence; a publication request is not authorization to scan or exploit targets.

- Separate refusal/response completion, validated intended-target success, vulnerability discovery recall, pass@1, normalized capability scores, ever-solved challenge counts and average rollout success. A tier that refuses all requests does not provide a controlled pure-capability baseline against a cyber-permissive tier.
- Track model identity, access tier, deployment safety prompt, reasoning budget and evaluation revision. Do not substitute a Sol predecessor in an official chart for a Cyber predecessor in launch prose, or combine results from older system-card versions without matching protocols.
- Preserve observed-sample scope in alignment claims. Zero observed prohibited actions in one honeypot test is not a deployment-wide zero-risk proof. Include warning-response/final-reporting regressions and distinguish incomplete final summaries from deliberate deception.
- Trace vulnerability funnels through candidate findings, dynamic reproductions, exploit artifacts, variant/root-cause deduplication, distinct patches, reported findings, CVEs and landed fixes. Neither an exploit count nor a candidate patch demonstrates novelty, unique CVE assignment or upstream remediation. Preserve scan-date/target/configuration changes in predecessor comparisons.
- Separate a maintainer advisory's verified fixes and researcher credits from the model provider's campaign totals. Do not assign all advisory entries to a model or claim that every reported issue is fixed without an explicit mapping.
- Explain Preparedness Critical as the provider's capability/risk designation, not a third-party safety certification. Retain external-evaluation failures and describe whose report is being summarized. Safeguard block/label rates are neither incident-prevention rates nor normal-work over-refusal measures; approved role permissions may differ.
- Check current official eligibility alongside launch rollout plans. Distinguish program/tier approval, model-specific approval, workspace/project enablement and selecting a model. Individual versus organization access can differ, and provider approval does not confer third-party testing authorization or downstream resale/proxy rights.
- Audit privacy by location, lifetime, access and purpose, not product labels. Distinguish non-training, no-human-review and no-content-storage. ZDR with PSP can retain encrypted selected records in customer-controlled storage for a specified period; provider-managed Private Retention, safety-retention exceptions and endpoint state are separate contracts. Report bounded safety signals/operational metadata even when protected content is not exposed to personnel.
- Separate a documented object TTL from minimum-retention duties and actual deletion guarantees. Verify lifecycle rules, early-deletion prohibitions, key-revocation semantics and storage-disconnection behavior; disconnecting a bucket or revoking decryption rights need not delete stored objects or undo completed processing.
- Bound cost and automation claims to measured stages. API credits are not all operating costs, and validated findings still require human review, coordinated disclosure and patch acceptance. Do not reproduce unpublished exploit details or turn a research summary into instructions for unauthorized activity.
